# V54: eye expression and den settling

The eye shader previously treated any downturned mouth as sadness. A mild frown, effort or alarm could therefore acquire the same worried upper cut. Worry now requires matching inner-brow lift, a meaningful negative mouth curve and low tension; alertness inhibits it. The CPU brow corrective follows the same distinction. This changes presentation, not saved mood or physiology.

Joy recruits the lower lid earlier along the continuous smile curve. Surprise can soften but no longer erase this cheek/lid lift. The cut remains a curved intrusion into the eye, and blinking keeps its independent control.

Validation: 270 body tests passed (three existing ignored), including a regression distinguishing small frowns, disappointment, tension and alertness. Clippy passed with warnings denied before the den follow-up. The production renderer generated 99 supplied-state captures: eleven expressions, three backgrounds and three scales. The reviewed comparison shows a visible lower cut for moderate joy, a stronger curve for strong joy and preserved sad/angry controls. Captures are not evidence of real-world expression frequency.

Live den diagnosis: a read-only telemetry lease showed SleepInDen still in Orient after 84.8 seconds, with zero velocity. The body center at y=0.89841 could not reach the cushion target y=0.95088: its 0.05246 separation exceeded the old 0.04 center-arrival test despite the pet being seated. This kept requesting arrival rather than entering sleep.

The ecology frame now receives actual cradle admission, excludes dragging, and checks low speed and horizontal alignment before recognizing seated arrival. The semantic target remains the den anchor so downstream cradle navigation does not mistake rest for an exit request. A 120-second replay of the observed offset stays asleep; off-seat and wrong-side controls keep approaching. All 117 ecology tests passed, including these regressions. Final Clippy for app, body lab and habitat lab passed.

The motor support contract must recognize measured cradle seating independently of the screen edge; post-launch validation must inspect actual locomotion, not only the selected Sleep action.
