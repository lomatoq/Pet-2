## Material

The preserved Poisson volume and normal reconstruction remain. Continuous angular studio sources remove hard white card patches. Curved corneal reflections use the same studio and respect eyelid masks. The rejected angular-card candidate is not delivered.

The design draws on [Filament](https://google.github.io/filament/Filament.md.html) and [OpenPBR](https://academysoftwarefoundation.github.io/OpenPBR/); it is a bounded real-time approximation, not full GGX, spectral rendering or OpenPBR conformance. It adds no texture reads or render passes. Independent native GPU review accepts the third candidate; user aesthetic approval remains unestablished. Wake ablations preserve zero face/interior/leading changes and no wake at rest or stop. Submission plus GPU-readback timing is not GPU-only timing or whole-application FPS.
